# @uisatin/pro

## 1.0.0

### Minor Changes

- bc8c606: Activity timeline (`@uisatin/pro/activity-timeline`): a record's history, newest first, an ordered list under each day's name, in a group named "Activity" for screen readers. Each entry is the actor on a rail (an agent's is the tetris mark at rest, in a circle filled like Toast's and Attachment's icon tiles, with a 0.75px border/strong edge), **actor** verb **target**, and its time: relative today ("5 min ago"), kept current by one shared clock that renders only the times whose text changed; screen readers hear the full date. An agent's `changes` take `AgentSuggestionChange[]` and look as they do in AgentSuggestion. Runs of one actor fold into one row ("Agent made 4 changes") that opens onto them, controlled or not (`expanded` / `defaultExpanded` / `onExpandedChange`). `onRevert` adds Revert to `revertible` entries: it spins while the promise runs, then strikes the entry through with "Reverted by you", moves focus to it and shows a toast with Undo (`onUndoRevert`). A run opening or closing, and "Reverted by you" coming or going, change height over 300 ms on the sliding pill's curve: the rows below glide, the content fades in once the move starts, the chevron turns with it. New entries fade in at the top. Reduced motion makes it all instant. Loading and empty states, parts for a timeline of your own (`ActivityTimelineRoot`, `ActivityTimelineDay`, `ActivityTimelineList`, `ActivityTimelineEntry`, `ActivityTimelineRun`, `ActivityTimelineTime`), and its words (`activity`, `noActivity`, `activityRun`, `revert`, `revertChange`, `revertedByYou`, `revertedBy`, `changeReverted`, `undo`) translated with SatinLabels.
- 50c9104: Reviewable changes (`@uisatin/pro/agent-changes-review`), an AI pattern: the fields an agent wants to change, reviewed one at a time before any is applied. It's a DecisionCard with a change per step: `title` and `description` (the suggestion) and the "‹ 1/3 ›" navigator in the header, or each change's field as the title without one; the old value struck through, an arrow and the new one, then, when the change has a `source` (`{ icon, label, quote }`), a preview of where it comes from: a 16px logo, "Transcription" and the quoted line that justifies the change. The footer is DecisionCard's: Skip and Accept. Either decides the change and slides in the next one still pending; deciding the last one finishes the review. The navigator goes back to decide a change again, and a change you come back to says "Accepted" or "Skipped" under it; the last decision goes straight to applying, with no tick. It takes the same `changes` as AgentSuggestionChanges. On finishing, `onApply` gets the accepted changes only; a promise holds the applying stage (buttons locked, a Spinner on the one pressed), then the card fades out and `onApplied` fires for the app to remove it (focus goes to `returnFocus()` or the page, and "2 changes applied" is read out from a live region on the body). Controlled or not (`value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange`), `defaultStep` to open on another change. Its buttons are named after the change ("Accept Stage change", "Skip Stage change"). Its words (`acceptFieldChange`, `acceptFieldChangeText`, `skipFieldChange`, `previousFieldChange`, `nextFieldChange`, `fieldChangeAccepted`, `fieldChangeSkipped`, `applyingFieldChanges`, `fieldChangesApplied`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- da2b662: Permission request, a Satin Pro AI pattern (`@uisatin/pro/agent-permissions`): what an agent will touch before it first runs. The agent's avatar sits in a tile of its tone, over a soft aura of that tone, with the title and reason centred under it; then one row per scope with a plain verb ("Read candidates", "Send emails") and a Checkbox; scopes that send stand apart with an amber tile and "· Sends as you" after the title. Don't allow, Allow once, or Always allow from the SplitButton's menu; unticking scopes makes a partial request. It is the question only: `onAllow` gets the mode and the ticked scopes, and what follows an answer (closing the Dialog, granting the access) is the app's part; a promise from `onAllow` or `onDeny` shows a Spinner until it settles. Inside a Dialog focus starts on Don't allow. Its words (`agentPermissions*`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- 3dec748: Agent plan (`@uisatin/pro/agent-plan`): the steps an agent proposes before it acts. In a draft each step shows its number, its label, and under the label one muted line with its tool (the app's logo from `toolIcon`, an `img` at 16px or an icon, then the name) and detail. Pressing a step opens Rename and Remove under it; Rename turns the label into a field. Dragging the number or Alt+↑/↓ reorders it: a dragged step lifts and follows the pointer, the others make room, and the number shows a grip on hover or keyboard focus. Run plan is disabled while the plan is empty. Once it runs, the steps are drawn like Thinking's: the current one shows AgentLoading, a finished step's check fades in, a failed one shows its error with Retry and Skip. One polite live region reads out each step as it finishes. Parts: `AgentPlanRoot`, `AgentPlanHeader`, `AgentPlanSteps`, `AgentPlanActions`. Its words join SatinLabels.
- 88adf6f: Agent presence (`@uisatin/pro/agent-presence`): shows which row or card an agent is editing right now. `<AgentPresence target agent label />` lays an edge in the agent's tone and a small label (its mark playing and its name) over the target, without touching it, so nothing moves; `activity` ("Drafting invite…") puts the step the agent is on in the label, in place of its name; it follows the target's corner radius and stops at whatever clips it. It fades in over 150 ms and glides to the next target on `--ease-in-out`; it follows its own target through scrolls, sorts and a card moving column. States: `working`, `done` (green with a check, then it fades out after `doneDuration`) and `conflict` (amber, dashed, with the person's avatar: someone is editing the same row). Under reduced motion nothing fades or glides and the mark stands still. Changes are read out from a polite status region ("Satin is updating Tara Mendes"). `useAgentPresence()` keeps where one agent is in a list: `item(id)` for each Table row (through `renderRow`) or Kanban card, `start(id, label, activity?)`, `done`, `conflict`, `clear`, and `props` for the component; controlled with `value` / `onValueChange` or not. The agent can be AgentPicker's `Agent`. Parts: `AgentPresenceRoot`, `AgentPresenceOutline`, `AgentPresenceLabel`. Its words (`agentPresenceWorking`, `agentPresenceActivity`, `agentPresenceDone`, `agentPresenceConflict`, `agentPresenceEditing`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- c32bf2e: Agent action log (`@uisatin/pro/agent-undo-log`), an AI pattern for undo over confirm, made for coding agents first: `<AgentActionLog runs onRevert onRevertRun onRestore />` lists what an agent did, run by run, with a Revert on every action. A run is `{ id, label, at, actions }` on a rail like Thinking's steps, its header counting the actions and the lines added and removed. An action is one line (`label` "Edited", `target` in mono, `added` / `removed` in green and red) with a quiet Revert icon button and a tooltip; one that can't be reverted (`revertible: false`, a push) shows a lock and says why (`reason`). While `onRevert`'s promise runs the button shows a Spinner; done, the line strikes through and fades to muted and a toast says so, with Undo that calls `onRestore` (the line offers Restore too). A rejected revert marks the line "Couldn't revert" with the Error's message, and Try again. Revert run and ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z off Apple; `shortcut="document" | "log" | "off"`) revert what is left of the newest run, newest action first. `reverted` and `expanded` are controlled or not; `changes` (AgentSuggestionChanges) and `description` serve agents outside code. Parts: `AgentActionLogRoot`, `AgentActionLogRun`, `AgentActionLogAction`. Its words (`actionLog…`, `undo`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- 075104d: Bulk action bar, the first Satin Pro component (`@uisatin/pro/bulk-action-bar`): the toolbar that appears when rows are selected. A select-all checkbox (with `total` and `onSelectAll`: indeterminate while some are selected, one click selects all), the count in a grey Badge (its digits roll as it changes, except under reduced motion), the actions as buttons and the rest in a ⋯ menu, then the clear button at the end, after a rule. An action that returns a promise shows a Spinner, then a checkmark. Escape clears the selection while focus is in the table or the bar; arrow keys move along the toolbar. `RollingNumber` (`@uisatin/pro/rolling-number`) is the rolling count on its own. It sticks to the bottom of its container and keeps its place while hidden, so it never covers the last row. Works with Table's `selected` / `onSelectedChange` as they are. Its words (`bulkActions`, `bulkSelected`, `selectAll`, `clearSelection`, `selectionCleared`, `moreActions`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- e7278a4: Command pages (`@uisatin/pro/command-pages`): the ⌘K Command menu with sub-pages and an agent for what no command matches. An item's `page` (or `onSelect(menu)` → `menu.push(page)`) opens a page in place of the list, with a Breadcrumb above the search; Backspace in the empty search goes back one page and puts back the search you had there (a held key goes back only once), and the footer says so ("⌫ Back"). With `onAsk`, every query adds 'Ask the agent about "…"' as the last row; it opens the agent's page, where the question sits over `answer` (StreamingText) and `answering` (AgentLoading until the first words), and the search asks follow-ups. A group's `icon` is the kind of record it lists (a person, a job, a company) and shows on every row without its own. An item's `success` ("Moved to Negotiation") shows as a success toast once it has run, after the promise its `onSelect` returns. The panel keeps the top edge it opened with, so a page of another length grows or shrinks it downwards. Page to page it moves as Decision card's steps do (the shared step slide): the new page comes in a whole menu's width from the end side going in (the start side coming back) and fades in, the old one goes out the other way, and the list's box eases to the new height, 360 ms; nothing moves under reduced motion. The breadcrumb row is there on the first page too, so the search and its icon never move from page to page. `controlRef` opens it on a page, a search or an answer from outside. Part: `CommandPagesAnswer`. Its words (`commandAskAbout`, `commandAskAgent`, `commandAskFollowUp`, `commandBack`, `commandPageOpened`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- de58e49: Data grid (`@uisatin/pro/data-grid`): the table people work in, on the APG Grid pattern. One cell at a time is in the tab order; the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down move it (mirrored right to left). Typed columns (`key` types the value `renderCell` and `renderEditor` get; `computed: true` for a column of your own) with `width`, `minWidth`, `maxWidth`, `pinned: 'start' | 'end'`, `sortable`, `hideable`, `editable` and `align`. Sort, selection, column visibility, order and sizing each work controlled or uncontrolled. Enter, F2 or a double click edits a cell (an Input, or your `renderEditor`); Enter or Tab saves through `onCellEdit`, which may return a promise: the cell shows a Spinner while it runs and an error edge with its message if it rejects (Enter tries again, Escape drops it). Columns resize by dragging a header's edge, following the pointer with no transition, or from the keyboard (Tab from the header to its handle, then the arrow keys). Pinned columns cast a soft shadow once content scrolls under them. Above 200 rows only the rows on screen are drawn, with `aria-rowcount` for the rest. `density="compact"`, `loading` (Skeleton rows), empty, `filtered` (No results, with Clear filters), `error` (with Try again) and `pageSize` (Pagination under the grid). The `toolbar` slot takes `DataGridToolbar`, `DataGridColumnsMenu` (show, hide and, with Alt and the arrow keys, reorder columns) and `DataGridViewMenu` (saved views of the sort, the columns and your filters; a switch fades the body in over --duration-base, and a Dot marks unsaved changes). The empty, no-results and error states are EmptyStates with their icons, centred in the visible body however far the columns scroll; `noResults` names your own search. Its words are translated with SatinLabels.
- 904db15: Availability calendar (`@uisatin/pro/date-availability`): `AvailabilityCalendar` and `AvailabilityDatePicker` show each day's free slots from `availability` (keyed by ISO date) with a green dot (a ring at `fewSlots` or fewer) and a tooltip ("3 slots"), said in the day's name too. Full days fade and can't be picked but stay reachable with the arrow keys, their reason ("Fully booked", or the app's `reason`) in the tooltip and the name. `useAvailability()` gives the `isDisabled` and `renderDay` for any Calendar or picker. Words: `availabilitySlots`, `availabilityFreeSlots`, `availabilityFull`, `availabilityDay`.
- 87ded0b: Filter bar (`@uisatin/pro/filter-bar`): one facet per field, each in a fixed place, idle ("Stage") or set ("Owner: Me", with a × that clears it). No "+ Filter" and no operators: a facet opens its choices. `enum`, `person` and `multi` fields open a menu of checkboxes that stays open; a person field lists `me` first, as "Me", and options with a `tone` show as Badges, in the menu and in the facet, two at most, then "+N". Number fields open a menu of presets ("> €20k", "€10k – €50k"); date fields open presets ("This quarter", "Last 30 days") and Custom range…, a Calendar on the same surface. Clear filters clears them all, and the result count rolls and is read out as it changes. The value is plain JSON, one filter per field; `applyFilters(rows, value, fields)` filters rows for a List, Table or DataGrid, and a filter no facet can say stays idle and doesn't apply. Its words (`filterBar`, `filterFacetValue`, `filterClearField`, `filterClearAll`, `filterComparison`, `filterThis`, `filterLastDays`, `filterCustomRange`, `filterResults`…) are translated with SatinLabels.
- fafb4f2: Kanban board (`@uisatin/pro/kanban-board`): columns of cards, each with its count and an optional limit (the count turns amber past it), and cards that move between stages by mouse, touch (after a 250 ms hold, so a swipe still scrolls) or keyboard (Space picks up, arrows move between places and columns, Space drops, Escape cancels). The lifted card scales to 1.02 and follows the pointer, a dashed gap opens where it will land, the other cards shift on --ease-in-out and the drop settles on --ease-out. It is controlled: a move only calls `onMove(itemId, toColumn, index)`, and `moveKanbanItem()` does the splice; `index` counts loaded cards only. A move to another column shows a "Moved to Negotiation" toast whose Undo puts the card back in its exact place.

  A column's `more` counts cards `items` doesn't hold yet: the count and the limit include them, and the column ends with "+2 more", a button when `onShowMore` is set. `canMove(item, column)` refuses a column, with a reason when it returns a string: from the moment a card lifts, that column's cards dim, the reason shows in a pill under the lifted card, and a drop there glides the card back and reads the reason out; the ⋯ menu lists the column with its reason. `confirmMove(item, column, index)` runs before `onMove` (Undo skips it): `false` puts the card back, and a promise keeps it at its new place, at 0.6 opacity and `aria-busy`, until it settles, so the app can ask in a Dialog.

  `KanbanCard` is laid out like a sales pipeline's lead: a `logo` and `title` (a Link), a `status` Badge under it, `meta`, labelled `fields` (`{ icon, label, value, placeholder }`, a description list), then a bottom row with the `footer`, the `age` (an amber Badge with an alert clock when `stale`) and an `avatar`. When its `changed` key changes, the card lights up once (opacity only, in 150 ms, out 250 ms, not under reduced motion) and the board reads "Northwind Labs was updated". It has a ⋯ menu that moves the card without dragging. Collapsed columns (`collapsedColumns`, `defaultCollapsedColumns`, `collapsible`), `onAddItem`, `loading` with Skeleton cards and an EmptyState for empty columns. Every move is read out from a live region ("Moved Northwind Labs to Qualified, position 6 of 6"), and its words (`kanbanCard`, `kanbanCardHint`, `kanbanCount`, `kanbanEmpty`, `kanbanAddTo`, `kanbanCollapse`, `kanbanExpand`, `kanbanCardMenu`, `kanbanMoveTo`, `kanbanMoveUp`, `kanbanMoveDown`, `kanbanPickedUp`, `kanbanOver`, `kanbanMoved`, `kanbanCancelled`, `kanbanMovedTo`, `kanbanMore`, `kanbanRefused`, `kanbanStale`, `kanbanUpdated`, `undo`) are translated with SatinLabels.

- 20755e0: People picker (`@uisatin/pro/people-picker`): Combobox for people. Rows show a 20px avatar, the name and the role (or email), read as "Marta Nowak, Hiring manager"; picks show as chips with avatars, folded into "+N" after `maxChips`. People come from `people`, titled `groups`, or `search(query, { signal })`, debounced (`searchDelay`, 150 ms), with stale requests aborted; while it runs, the last results stay on screen narrowed by the query, a Spinner shows only for answers later than 300 ms, and a failure says so. `people` and `groups` filter as you type, with no delay. `recent` is listed first while the query is empty. A `disabled` person is listed but can't be picked. Removing a chip moves focus to the next one, or to the field. Its words (`recentPeople`, `allPeople`, `addPeople`, `choosePerson`, `searchPeople`, `searchingPeople`, `noPeopleFound`, `peopleSearchFailed`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- 297556b: Review queue (`@uisatin/pro/review-queue`): agent drafts gone through one at a time, Approve, Edit or Skip. Its place ("4 of 12") sits over a bar that fills as items are decided; the next item slides 8px in. J and K move, A, E and S act, but not while typing in a field; the place is read out as it changes. Edit opens the item's fields in a Sheet and brings you back to the same item. At the end, a summary lists them one under another (`itemMedia` puts an Avatar beside each name), with a SegmentedControl between Approved and Skipped, and Review skipped to go round again. Parts (`ReviewQueueRoot`, `ReviewQueueHeader`, `ReviewQueueItem`, `ReviewQueueActions`, `ReviewQueueSummary`, `useReviewQueue`) for a layout of your own; its words (`review*`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- ae5b03f: Scheduler (`@uisatin/pro/scheduler`): day, week and month views of events over time slots, in a given `timeZone` (DST-safe, with `zonedDate()` / `zonedParts()` for the app). Drag an event to move it (it settles into its slot with --ease-out) or its bottom edge to change the end; Alt and the arrow keys do the same from the keyboard. Overlapping events sit side by side; an all-day row, a now line, free and busy slots (`availability`), working hours and read-only events. Picking slots calls `onSlotSelect`; `eventPopover` and `slotPopover` fill a popover. The week starts where `dateLocale` says, and every date and time is written in it. Its words (`scheduler*`) are translated with SatinLabels.
- 49564a5: ScopedSearch (`@uisatin/pro/search-scoped`): one search box over several kinds of record or places, with a scope chip in the field ("Everywhere ▾", "Candidates ▾", "This project ▾") that says where it looks. `scopes` are data: `groups` keeps some kinds of record, `fields` keeps the rows of one place, the first is the default. Pressing the chip or typing `in:` lists the scopes with how many results each holds for the query, and a scope that finds nothing offers the ones that do. `filters` narrow inside the scope as typed `key:value` chips (`stage:interview`). Recent searches, results grouped with counts, Backspace removes the chips and then the scope, ⌘K focuses it, Escape closes the list. `parseScopedQuery`, `formatScopedQuery` and `filterScopedGroups` read, write and filter a query `{ scope, tokens, text }` for a server search.
- 0407679: Grouped table (`@uisatin/pro/table-pro`): `TableGrouped` is Table with rows that open onto sub-rows (`getSubRows`, `expanded`), groups with a header, a count and subtotals (`groupBy`, `groupOrder`, `renderGroup`, `collapsedGroups`), columns that `format` and `aggregate` their numbers, a totals row in a `tfoot` (`footer`), a pinned first column with a shadow (`pinned="start"`) and `density="compact"`. With sub-rows or groups it is a treegrid with arrow-key row navigation. Part: `TableRowToggle`. New labels `tableRowsUnder`, `tableGroupCount`, `tableSelectGroup`.

- 083c262: Dark surfaces lose their blue cast at the same lightness: `bg/canvas` #09090A, `bg/card` #121213, `bg/field` #1A1A1B, `bg/popover` #222223, `bg/muted`, `bg/hover`, `action/secondary/bg` and `status/neutral/bg` #272728, `bg/selected` and `border/default` #2F2F31, `border/subtle` #262628 (new primitives `--sa-color-canvas-dark` and `--sa-color-muted-dark`; Light is unchanged). They no longer read `--sa-effect-shadow-sm`, so an app that restyled menu shadows through it should set the two new variables instead.
