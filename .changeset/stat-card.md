Stat card (`@uisatin/pro/stat-card`): the first tile on a dashboard, small enough for a four-up row at 240px. The label with the change's period at the row's end, the value with its change as a small Badge (an arrow and the percent, green when it moved the `good` way, red when not, grey when flat; `good: 'down'` for CAC, refund rate, time to hire), and a sparkline drawn with Line chart's `smoothPath` in chart/3 over its gradient, edge to edge: from the card's left edge to its right, its gradient down to the bottom edge. Its dot and tooltip glide after the pointer (Free's `useGlide`). The accessible name says it in words ("Signups, 1,284, up 12% vs last week", ", good" or ", bad" only when down is good), and the sparkline is summed up in a sentence. Without `href` the sparkline is a tab stop whose arrow keys pick a point (dot, tooltip, live region); with `href` the whole card is one link. `loading` keeps the loaded height, `error` shows the reason and Retry, `value={null}` shows "—", `disabled` fades it. A number counts up once (250 ms), never under reduced motion. Parts: `StatCardChange`, `StatCardSparkline`. Words in SatinLabels (`statUp`, `statDown`, `statFlat`, `statGood`, `statBad`, `statNoValue`, `statLoading`, `statError`, `statRetry`, `statTrend`, `statPoints`, `statPoint`).
