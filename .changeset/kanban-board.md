Kanban board (`@uisatin/pro/kanban-board`): columns of cards, each with its count and an optional limit (the count turns amber past it), and cards that move between stages by mouse, touch (after a 250 ms hold, so a swipe still scrolls) or keyboard (Space picks up, arrows move between places and columns, Space drops, Escape cancels). The lifted card scales to 1.02 and follows the pointer, a dashed gap opens where it will land, the other cards shift on --ease-in-out and the drop settles on --ease-out. It is controlled: a move only calls `onMove(itemId, toColumn, index)`, and `moveKanbanItem()` does the splice; `index` counts loaded cards only. A move to another column shows a "Moved to Negotiation" toast whose Undo puts the card back in its exact place.

A column's `more` counts cards `items` doesn't hold yet: the count and the limit include them, and the column ends with "+2 more", a button when `onShowMore` is set. `canMove(item, column)` refuses a column, with a reason when it returns a string: from the moment a card lifts, that column's cards dim, the reason shows in a pill under the lifted card, and a drop there glides the card back and reads the reason out; the ⋯ menu lists the column with its reason. `confirmMove(item, column, index)` runs before `onMove` (Undo skips it): `false` puts the card back, and a promise keeps it at its new place, at 0.6 opacity and `aria-busy`, until it settles, so the app can ask in a Dialog.

`KanbanCard` is laid out like a sales pipeline's lead: a `logo` and `title` (a Link), a `status` Badge under it, `meta`, labelled `fields` (`{ icon, label, value, placeholder }`, a description list), then a bottom row with the `footer`, the `age` (an amber Badge with an alert clock when `stale`) and an `avatar`. When its `changed` key changes, the card lights up once (opacity only, in 150 ms, out 250 ms, not under reduced motion) and the board reads "Northwind Labs was updated". It has a ⋯ menu that moves the card without dragging. Collapsed columns (`collapsedColumns`, `defaultCollapsedColumns`, `collapsible`), `onAddItem`, `loading` with Skeleton cards and an EmptyState for empty columns. Every move is read out from a live region ("Moved Northwind Labs to Qualified, position 6 of 6"), and its words (`kanbanCard`, `kanbanCardHint`, `kanbanCount`, `kanbanEmpty`, `kanbanAddTo`, `kanbanCollapse`, `kanbanExpand`, `kanbanCardMenu`, `kanbanMoveTo`, `kanbanMoveUp`, `kanbanMoveDown`, `kanbanPickedUp`, `kanbanOver`, `kanbanMoved`, `kanbanCancelled`, `kanbanMovedTo`, `kanbanMore`, `kanbanRefused`, `kanbanStale`, `kanbanUpdated`, `undo`) are translated with SatinLabels.
