Kanban board (`@uisatin/pro/kanban-board`): columns of cards, each with its count and an optional limit (the count turns amber past it), and cards that move between stages by mouse, touch (after a 250 ms hold, so a swipe still scrolls) or keyboard (Space picks up, arrows move between places and columns, Space drops, Escape cancels). The lifted card scales to 1.02 and follows the pointer, a dashed gap opens where it will land, the other cards shift on --ease-in-out and the drop settles on --ease-out. It is controlled: a move only calls `onMove(itemId, toColumn, index)`, and `moveKanbanItem()` does the splice. A move to another column shows a "Moved to Interview" toast whose Undo puts the card back in its exact place. `KanbanCard` (title, meta, badges, avatar) has a ⋯ menu that moves the card without dragging. Collapsed columns (`collapsedColumns`, `defaultCollapsedColumns`, `collapsible`), `onAddItem`, `loading` with Skeleton cards and an EmptyState for empty columns. Every move is read out from a live region ("Moved Tara Mendes to Interview, position 2 of 7"), and its words (`kanbanCard`, `kanbanCardHint`, `kanbanCount`, `kanbanEmpty`, `kanbanAddTo`, `kanbanCollapse`, `kanbanExpand`, `kanbanCardMenu`, `kanbanMoveTo`, `kanbanMoveUp`, `kanbanMoveDown`, `kanbanPickedUp`, `kanbanOver`, `kanbanMoved`, `kanbanCancelled`, `kanbanMovedTo`, `undo`) are translated with SatinLabels.
