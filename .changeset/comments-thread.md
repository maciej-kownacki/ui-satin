Comments thread (`@uisatin/pro/comments-thread`): a discussion on a record, with replies one level deep (a reply to a reply joins its root, an orphan becomes a root), @mentions and resolve. Each comment is an `<article>` named "Comment by Marta Nowak, {full date}": avatar, name, Free's `RelativeTime`, "(edited)", the body with its line breaks and its `mentions` drawn as Free's `Mention`. The composer is a Satin Textarea with Prompt bar mentions (`mentions.search`); Ctrl/⌘+Enter sends, Enter is a new line. `onReply(parentId, body, mentions)` is optimistic when it returns a promise: a dimmed row with a Spinner until the comment arrives in `comments`, which takes its place without fading in again; a rejection leaves "Not sent" with Retry (focused) and Discard. Edit happens in place and stays optimistic until the body changes; a failed `onEdit` reopens the editor with the text and an error. Delete (your own, anyone's with `moderator`) never asks: the comment goes at once, focus moves on, and a toast offers Undo with a countdown; `onDelete` runs when the toast closes without Undo, or at once on unmount or `pagehide`, and a failure brings the comment back with an error toast (needs the app's `<Toaster />`). `resolvable` adds Resolve at the start of the composer's row; a resolved thread folds to one line ("Resolved by …", Show comments, Reopen) with opacity only, controlled or not (`resolved` / `defaultResolved` / `onResolve`, `resolvedBy`). New comments fade in 4px up; reduced motion turns it off. Empty state (or `empty`), `disabled` for read only, parts (`Comment`, `CommentComposer`, `CommentActions`), and its words (`comments…`/`comment…` keys in `commentsThreadLabels`, Send from Free) translated with SatinLabels.
