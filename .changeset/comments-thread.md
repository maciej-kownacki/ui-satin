Comments thread (`@uisatin/pro/comments-thread`): a discussion on a record, with replies one level deep (a reply to a reply joins its root, an orphan becomes a root), @mentions and resolve. Each comment is an `<article>` named "Comment by Marta Nowak, {full date}": avatar, name, Free's `RelativeTime`, "(edited)", the body with its line breaks and its `mentions` drawn as Free's `Mention` with a tooltip of the person's avatar and full name; bodies are markdown-lite (`**bold**`, `_italic_`, `~~struck~~`, `` `code` ``, `[text](https://…)` for http, https and mailto only, and `- ` lists; never HTML), and an author with `kind: 'agent'` shows the agent mark and an "Agent" Badge; replies hang off a thread line, your own comments say "(me)" and show Edit next to Reply, and `title` (with `titleAs`) adds a heading with the count in a Badge, `variant="card"` a card around it. The composer is a 72px Satin Textarea with Prompt bar mentions (`mentions.search`, whose `avatar` travels with the mention) and a formatting toolbar (Bold, Italic, Code, Bulleted list, Link; ⌘/Ctrl+B, I, E, K) that writes the marks through the field's own undo and never splits a mention; Ctrl/⌘+Enter sends, Enter is a new line. Reply, Edit, Retry and Discard carry icons. `onReply(parentId, body, mentions)` is optimistic when it returns a promise: a dimmed row with a Spinner until the comment arrives in `comments`, which takes its place without fading in again; a rejection leaves "Not sent" with Retry (focused) and Discard. Edit happens in place and stays optimistic until the body changes; a failed `onEdit` reopens the editor with the text and an error. Delete (your own, anyone's with `moderator`) never asks: the comment goes at once, focus moves on, and a toast offers Undo with a countdown; `onDelete` runs when the toast closes without Undo, or at once on unmount or `pagehide`, and a failure brings the comment back with an error toast (needs the app's `<Toaster />`). `resolvable` adds Resolve at the start of the composer's row; a resolved thread folds to one line ("Resolved by …", Show comments, Reopen) with opacity only, controlled or not (`resolved` / `defaultResolved` / `onResolve`, `resolvedBy`). New comments fade in 4px up; reduced motion turns it off. Empty state (or `empty`), `disabled` for read only, parts (`Comment`, `CommentComposer`, `CommentActions`), and its words (`comments…`/`comment…` keys in `commentsThreadLabels`, Send, Cancel and Sending from Free) translated with SatinLabels.
