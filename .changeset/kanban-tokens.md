A component token for Satin Pro's Kanban board: `--component-kanban-column-bg`, the top of a column's wash, which fades to transparent down the column. It is bg/canvas in Light and bg/card in Dark: one step under the field surface the board sits on, and under the cards' bg/popover. The contrast check holds the column's text and the focus ring to it.
