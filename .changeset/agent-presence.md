Agent presence (`@uisatin/pro/agent-presence`): shows which row or card an agent is editing right now. `<AgentPresence target agent label />` lays an edge in the agent's tone and a small label (its mark playing and its name) over the target, without touching it, so nothing moves; it follows the target's corner radius and stops at whatever clips it. It fades in over 150 ms and glides to the next target on `--ease-in-out`; it follows its own target through scrolls, sorts and a card moving column. States: `working`, `done` (green with a check, then it fades out after `doneDuration`) and `conflict` (amber, dashed, with the person's avatar: someone is editing the same row). Under reduced motion nothing fades or glides and the mark stands still. Changes are read out from a polite status region ("Satin is updating Tara Mendes"). `useAgentPresence()` keeps where one agent is in a list: `item(id)` for each Table row (through `renderRow`) or Kanban card, `start`, `done`, `conflict`, `clear`, and `props` for the component; controlled with `value` / `onValueChange` or not. The agent can be AgentPicker's `Agent`. Parts: `AgentPresenceRoot`, `AgentPresenceOutline`, `AgentPresenceLabel`. Its words (`agentPresenceWorking`, `agentPresenceDone`, `agentPresenceConflict`, `agentPresenceEditing`) are translated with SatinLabels.
