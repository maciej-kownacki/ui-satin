Notification: a card for something the app did or heard about, with media on the left, a title and description, a badge, and a round dismiss button centred on its top-right corner. NotificationStack folds several into a pile, the first in front and two peeking below, and unfolds them one under another on hover, focus or a tap, the way Toast's stack does; a dismissed card fades out while the rest move up.
