New token `component/table-pro/total-bg` (`--sa-component-table-pro-total-bg`), the fill of the totals row in Satin Pro's grouped table: zinc-50 in Light, a step darker than the rows, and popover-dark in Dark, a step lighter, never the hover fill. The contrast check holds the row's text to 4.5:1 on it.
