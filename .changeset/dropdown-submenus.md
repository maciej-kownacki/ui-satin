DropdownMenu: submenus (`DropdownMenuSub`, `DropdownMenuSubTrigger`, `DropdownMenuSubContent`) and checkable rows (`DropdownMenuCheckboxItem`, `DropdownMenuRadioGroup`, `DropdownMenuRadioItem`), controlled or not. A submenu fades in from 4px on the side it opens. The menu now reads its direction from the trigger (or a new `dir` prop), so right to left ← opens a submenu and the panel is laid out right to left; a group with a header is named by it.
