Prompt mentions, for fields that aren't a textarea. `usePromptMenuState({ query, mentions, commands, disabled })` is the menu's state on its own: the host finds the trigger in its text and passes `{ kind, trigger, text, start }`; the hook searches (dropping a slow answer for an older query), filters and sorts commands, keeps the highlight, stays shut after Escape until the trigger changes, and says the number of suggestions in the status region. It returns `open`, `listed`, `items`, `index`, `current`, `move(±1)`, `highlight(i)`, `dismiss()`, `release(query)`, `announce(text)`, `listId`, `activeId` and `menuProps`. `PromptMentionsMenu` is the menu by itself (status region, group label, listbox, "No results", ↑↓ ↵ hints), anchored to `anchorRef` or a `virtualAnchor` rect, with `side`, `align`, `sideOffset` and `matchAnchorWidth` (false gives a fixed 288px). `usePromptMentions` and PromptBar are built on both and behave as before. `Mention` shows a mention outside a field, "@Tara Mendes" on a mention token's fill (status/brand-bg, a status/brand-border edge, radius-sm, medium text); its look is plain CSS on `[data-slot="mention"]`, so an editor that renders the same span without React gets it too.
