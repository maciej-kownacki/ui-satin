Activity timeline (`@uisatin/pro/activity-timeline`): a record's history, newest first, an ordered list under each day's name, in a group named "Activity" for screen readers. Each entry is the actor on a rail (an agent's is the tetris mark at rest), **actor** verb **target**, and its time: relative today ("5 min. ago"), kept current by one shared clock that renders only the times whose text changed; screen readers hear the full date. An agent's `changes` take `AgentSuggestionChange[]` and look as they do in AgentSuggestion. Runs of one actor fold into one row ("Agent made 4 changes") that opens onto them, controlled or not (`expanded` / `defaultExpanded` / `onExpandedChange`). `onRevert` adds Revert to `revertible` entries: it spins while the promise runs, then strikes the entry through with "Reverted by you", moves focus to it and shows a toast with Undo (`onUndoRevert`). A run opening or closing, and "Reverted by you" coming or going, change height over 300 ms on the sliding pill's curve: the rows below glide, the content fades in once the move starts, the chevron turns with it. New entries fade in at the top. Reduced motion makes it all instant. Loading and empty states, parts for a timeline of your own (`ActivityTimelineRoot`, `ActivityTimelineDay`, `ActivityTimelineList`, `ActivityTimelineEntry`, `ActivityTimelineRun`, `ActivityTimelineTime`), and its words (`activity`, `noActivity`, `activityRun`, `revert`, `revertChange`, `revertedByYou`, `revertedBy`, `changeReverted`, `undo`) translated with SatinLabels.
