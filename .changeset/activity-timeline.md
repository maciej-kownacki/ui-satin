Activity timeline (`@uisatin/pro/activity-timeline`): a record's history, newest first, an ordered list under each day's name, in a group named "Activity" for screen readers. Each entry is the actor on a rail (an agent's is the tetris mark at rest), **actor** verb **target**, and its time: relative today ("5 min. ago"), kept current by one shared clock that renders only the times whose text changed; screen readers hear the full date. An agent's `changes` take `AgentSuggestionChange[]` and look as they do in AgentSuggestion. Runs of one actor fold into one row ("Agent made 4 changes") that opens onto them, controlled or not (`expanded` / `defaultExpanded` / `onExpandedChange`). `onRevert` adds Revert to `revertible` entries: it spins while the promise runs, then strikes the entry through with "Reverted by you", moves focus to it and shows a toast with Undo (`onUndoRevert`). New entries and a run's entries fade in; nothing animates height. Loading and empty states, parts for a timeline of your own (`ActivityTimelineRoot`, `ActivityTimelineDay`, `ActivityTimelineList`, `ActivityTimelineEntry`, `ActivityTimelineRun`, `ActivityTimelineTime`), and its words (`activity`, `noActivity`, `activityRun`, `revert`, `revertChange`, `revertedByYou`, `revertedBy`, `changeReverted`, `undo`) translated with SatinLabels.
