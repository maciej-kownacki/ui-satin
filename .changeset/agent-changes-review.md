Reviewable changes (`@uisatin/pro/agent-changes-review`), an AI pattern: the fields an agent wants to change, reviewed one at a time before any is applied. It's a DecisionCard with a change per step: `title` and `description` (the suggestion) and the "‹ 1/3 ›" navigator in the header, or each change's field as the title without one; the old value struck through, an arrow and the new one, then, when the change has a `source` (`{ icon, label, quote }`), a preview of where it comes from: a 16px logo, "Transcription" and the quoted line that justifies the change; under it Dismiss (X) and Accept (a green check and "Accept"), both toggles. Deciding a change slides in the next one still pending, with DecisionCard's step motion; once none is pending, focus goes to Apply. The footer, where DecisionCard has Skip and Continue, holds Accept all and Apply ("Apply 2 changes"). It takes the same `changes` as AgentSuggestionChanges. `onApply` gets the accepted changes only; a promise holds the applying stage (decisions locked, Spinner), then the card fades out and `onApplied` fires for the app to remove it (focus goes to `returnFocus()` or the page, and "2 changes applied" is read out from a live region on the body). Controlled or not (`value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange`), `defaultStep` to open on another change. Its buttons are named after the change ("Accept Stage change", "Dismiss Stage change"). Its words (`acceptFieldChange`, `acceptFieldChangeText`, `rejectFieldChange`, `previousFieldChange`, `nextFieldChange`, `applyFieldChanges`, `applyingFieldChanges`, `fieldChangesApplied`, `allFieldChangesAccepted`) are translated with SatinLabels.
