Reviewable changes (`@uisatin/pro/agent-changes-review`), an AI pattern: the fields an agent wants to change, each accepted or rejected before any is applied. It takes the same `changes` as AgentSuggestionChanges and draws each as a card (component/changes-review/card-bg): the field's name on top, then the old value struck through, an arrow and the new one, then, when the change has a `source` (`{ icon, label, quote }`), a preview of where it comes from: a 16px logo, "Transcription" and the quoted line. At the card's bottom right, Dismiss (X) and Accept (a green check and "Accept"), both bordered xs buttons. Accept is a toggle (pressing it again makes the card pending). Dismiss slides the card out to the start while it fades, then moves the cards under it up into its place with a transform (no height animation; under reduced motion the card simply goes) and moves focus to the next card's Accept or the footer. Or a Checkbox before each card's name (`control="checkbox"`), where a cleared card stays and its value fades to text/muted. Then Accept all and Apply ("Apply 2 changes"). `onApply` gets the accepted changes only; a promise holds the applying stage (rows locked, Spinner), then Apply says Applied and each applied card gets a check. Controlled or not (`value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange`), with parts (`ReviewableChangesRoot`, `ReviewableChangesList`, `ReviewableChangesAcceptAll`, `ReviewableChangesApply`) to put Apply in AgentSuggestion's actions. Each card is a group named by its field and its buttons are named after the change ("Accept Stage change", "Dismiss Stage change"). Its words (`acceptFieldChange`, `acceptFieldChangeText`, `rejectFieldChange`, `applyFieldChanges`, `applyingFieldChanges`, `fieldChangesApplied`, `fieldChangeApplied`, `fieldChangeNotApplied`, `allFieldChangesAccepted`, `fieldChangeRejected`) are translated with SatinLabels.
