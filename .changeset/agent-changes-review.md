Reviewable changes (`@uisatin/pro/agent-changes-review`), an AI pattern: the fields an agent wants to change, reviewed one at a time before any is applied. It's a DecisionCard with a change per step: `title` and `description` (the suggestion) and the "‹ 1/3 ›" navigator in the header, or each change's field as the title without one; the old value struck through, an arrow and the new one, then, when the change has a `source` (`{ icon, label, quote }`), a preview of where it comes from: a 16px logo, "Transcription" and the quoted line that justifies the change. The footer is DecisionCard's: Skip and Accept. Either decides the change and slides in the next one still pending; deciding the last one finishes the review. The navigator goes back to decide a change again, and a change you come back to says "Accepted" or "Skipped" under it; the last decision goes straight to applying, with no tick. It takes the same `changes` as AgentSuggestionChanges. On finishing, `onApply` gets the accepted changes only; a promise holds the applying stage (buttons locked, a Spinner on the one pressed), then the card fades out and `onApplied` fires for the app to remove it (focus goes to `returnFocus()` or the page, and "2 changes applied" is read out from a live region on the body). Controlled or not (`value` / `defaultValue` / `onValueChange`), `defaultStep` to open on another change. Its buttons are named after the change ("Accept Stage change", "Skip Stage change"). Its words (`acceptFieldChange`, `acceptFieldChangeText`, `skipFieldChange`, `previousFieldChange`, `nextFieldChange`, `fieldChangeAccepted`, `fieldChangeSkipped`, `applyingFieldChanges`, `fieldChangesApplied`) are translated with SatinLabels.
