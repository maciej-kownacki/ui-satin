Funnel chart (`@uisatin/pro/funnel-chart`): a conversion funnel through stages (recruiting, sales, checkout) in the chart card frame. Each stage is a full-width row (or a column with `orientation="vertical"`) whose chart/3 bar is its share of the first stage, with its count, the conversion from the previous stage, from the first (`showConversion`: `'previous'`, `'first'`, `'both'`) and the drop-off. A drop-off of at least `dropOffWarning` (default 0.5) gets an amber Badge with a warning icon and the share lost, and "big drop-off" in the stage's name. A stage of 0 keeps its row. Bars fade in once from 95% of their length (opacity and transform, 250 ms), never under reduced motion or with `reducedMotion`. Hover and focus show the stage in Free's ChartTooltip, which glides after the pointer. The funnel is one tab stop: arrows, Home and End move between stages; with `onStageSelect` each stage is a button. Every stage is named in words ("Interview: 18, 30% of Screening, 7.5% of Applied, drop-off 42"), and a data table with the same rows is in the DOM (`showTable` shows it). `loading`, empty `stages` (`empty`) and `error` (with `onRetry`) keep the height of five stages. Counts and percentages are written in `dateLocale`; `format` writes counts another way. Stage `tone` takes Badge's tones. Parts: `FunnelChartStage`, `FunnelTable`, `funnelRows`. Words in SatinLabels (`funnelOf`, `funnelDropOff`, `funnelBigDropOff`, `funnelStageName`, `funnelNoData`, `funnelLoading`, `funnelError`, `funnelRetry`, `funnelStage`, `funnelCount`, `funnelFromPrevious`, `funnelFromFirst`, `funnelDropOffHead`).
