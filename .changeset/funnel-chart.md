Funnel chart (`@uisatin/pro/funnel-chart`): a conversion funnel through stages (recruiting, sales, checkout) in the chart card frame. The funnel is one chart/3 flow, its thickness each stage's share of the first stage to the power 0.35 (at least 3%), so the middle stages stay full and only a very small share draws thin, easing on an S-curve to the next stage's, its stages joined edge to edge (the stage on show lights up its area, the others fade): left to right with each stage's label and count on top, or top to bottom with `orientation="vertical"`. Each step sits under the piece of the flow that narrows, centred on the stage it leaves, with the share lost on the way ("↓ 75%"); `showConversion` (`'previous'`, the default, `'first'`, `'both'`) can add each count's share of the first or swap the steps for it. A drop-off of at least `dropOffWarning` (default 0.5) puts "big drop-off" in the stage's name and the table. The tooltip is a card beside the stage, off its words: the count and the drop-off as rows, the conversions in a muted line. The last stage closes the flow to a sharp, hyperbolic point. A stage of 0 keeps its column. Each stage's shape fades in once from 95% of its thickness (opacity and transform, 250 ms), never under reduced motion or with `reducedMotion`. Hover and focus show the stage in Free's ChartTooltip, which glides after the pointer. The funnel is one tab stop: arrows, Home and End move between stages; with `onStageSelect` each stage is a button. Every stage is named in words ("Interview: 18, 30% of Screening, 7.5% of Applied, drop-off 42"), and a data table with the same rows is in the DOM (`showTable` shows it). `loading`, empty `stages` (`empty`) and `error` (with `onRetry`) keep the height of five stages. Counts and percentages are written in `dateLocale`; `format` writes counts another way. Stage `tone` takes Badge's tones. Parts: `FunnelChartStage`, `FunnelTable`, `funnelRows`, `funnelShapePath`. Words in SatinLabels (`funnelOf`, `funnelDropOff`, `funnelBigDropOff`, `funnelStageName`, `funnelNoData`, `funnelLoading`, `funnelError`, `funnelRetry`, `funnelStage`, `funnelCount`, `funnelFromPrevious`, `funnelFromFirst`, `funnelDropOffHead`).
