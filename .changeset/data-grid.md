Data grid (`@uisatin/pro/data-grid`): the table people work in, on the APG Grid pattern. One cell at a time is in the tab order; the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down move it (mirrored right to left). Typed columns (`key` types the value `renderCell` and `renderEditor` get; `computed: true` for a column of your own) with `width`, `minWidth`, `maxWidth`, `pinned: 'start' | 'end'`, `sortable`, `hideable`, `editable` and `align`. Sort, selection, column visibility, order and sizing each work controlled or uncontrolled. Enter, F2 or a double click edits a cell (an Input, or your `renderEditor`); Enter or Tab saves through `onCellEdit`, which may return a promise: the cell shows a Spinner while it runs and an error edge with its message if it rejects (Enter tries again, Escape drops it). Columns resize by dragging a header's edge, following the pointer with no transition, or from the keyboard (Tab from the header to its handle, then the arrow keys). Pinned columns cast a soft shadow once content scrolls under them. Above 200 rows only the rows on screen are drawn, with `aria-rowcount` for the rest. `density="compact"`, `loading` (Skeleton rows), empty, `filtered` (No results, with Clear filters), `error` (with Try again) and `pageSize` (Pagination under the grid). The `toolbar` slot takes `DataGridToolbar`, `DataGridColumnsMenu` (show, hide and, with Alt and the arrow keys, reorder columns) and `DataGridViewMenu` (saved views of the sort, the columns and your filters; a switch fades the body in over --duration-base, and a Dot marks unsaved changes). Its words are translated with SatinLabels.
